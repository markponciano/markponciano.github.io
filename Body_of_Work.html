What I build into every system

01

Data you can trust

Every record lives in one place, and every other tool updates from it. So they all show the same numbers.

Proof: the website, GoHighLevel, Xero, the calendar, email and documents, all on one database.

02

The wrong button isn't there

Fix the screen, not the people.

Proof: salespeople get four steps and nothing that breaks a quote.

03

You know the moment something breaks

Silent failures cost the most: the email never sent, the invoice never raised. I watch for them, so the right person knows straight away.

Proof: a monitoring screen that watches the connections and deliveries, and an outside service that checks the monitoring itself is still running.

04

Nobody gets more access than their job needs

People and software alike.

Proof: salespeople see one screen, and each connected app gets only the access it needs.

05

Every change leaves a trail

Who did what, and when. When something goes wrong, you have a record, not an argument.

Proof: every money or date change the team makes is logged with who made it, and so is every change to the system's settings.

06

The tool fits the job

Ready-made or custom, whichever lasts.

Proof: 37 tools checked before building custom. Seasonal payroll left in Google Sheets.

07

Carefully planned changes, so the business never stops

Every change is checked before it goes live, with a way back at every step.

Proof: the whole system switched over in a single day, after about 130 checks, while the business kept trading. The team had a live training session before the switch.

08

Nothing lives only in my head

Everything is written down, so the business never depends on me remembering.

Proof: the booking system, written down end to end so someone else can run it. Written guides and walkthrough videos, so the team runs what I build without me.
